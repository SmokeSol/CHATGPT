# AGENT SOCIETY V2 — RUNBOOK

## Objective

Test whether an empirically anchored synthetic electorate whose individual transition decisions are generated by a frozen LLM adds out-of-sample predictive information beyond the structural baseline.

This is a forecast experiment, not a political persuasion system and not a narrative simulation.

## The experiment in one sentence

For election `t`, reconstruct a weighted electorate that exactly reproduces election `t-1`, give each synthetic voter only pre-election information for `t`, let the frozen LLM decide turnout and party probabilities, aggregate the ballots, and compare the result with the structural baseline.

## What is different from the old Agent Society

The old pilot calibrated aggregate agents back to the target-era result and used synthetic local demographic assumptions. V2 forbids both.

V2 starts from the PREVIOUS election only. The target election is never used to construct, calibrate or repair the society.

- Information: facts published before the 2016 cutoff.

- Role: retrospective benchmark only.

### 2021

- Political anchor: 2016 vote + abstention.
- Demography: RGPH 2014 plus sourceable pre-2021 electorate adjustments.
- Information: facts published before the 2021 cutoff.
- Output: simulated 2021 vote.
- Role: retrospective benchmark only. It is NOT a pristine holdout because its outcome has already been unsealed to the orchestrator.

### 2026

- Political anchor: 2021 vote + abstention.
- Demography: RGPH 2024 plus the frozen 2026 electorate denominator model.
- Information: only evidence available before the 2026 freeze date.
- Output: immutable `ASV2-2026` forecast.
- Role: decisive prospective falsification.

## Voter construction

Target 128 weighted archetypes per constituency.

Every archetype contains only sourceable attributes:

- age band;
- sex;
- urban/rural;
- education band;
- activity/employment status;
- previous-election state: one party or ABSTAIN.

Do not manufacture ideology, partisan identity or demographic-party correlations.

Weights must satisfy two separate constraints:

1. demographic marginals;
2. exact previous-election turnout and party-share masses.

Where joint demographic distributions are unavailable, use maximum entropy rather than invented correlation.

## LLM task

For each voter archetype, using the same frozen prompt:

1. estimate `P(turnout)`;
2. conditional on turnout, return a probability simplex over the available anonymous parties;
3. cite only a closed list of supplied evidence-family reason codes.

No free-form political narrative is required or scored.

Historical party, candidate and territory identities are blinded. The judge sees structured attributes and pre-election evidence, not identifying labels that could trigger memorized election results.

## Experimental arms

### C0_STRUCTURAL

The frozen structural baseline. F0 in 2026.

### AS1_PRIOR_ONLY

The synthetic society with no LLM transition. Previous vote/abstention is carried forward. This tests whether population construction itself changes anything.

### AS2_LLM_INDEPENDENT — PRIMARY

The LLM transitions voters independently from their previous state using demographics plus true pre-election context.

### AS2_SHUFFLED_CONTEXT — NEGATIVE CONTROL

Same voters and same amount of evidence, but candidate/event context is deterministically shuffled across comparable territories/parties.

If this performs as well as or better than true-context AS2, the mechanism is not credible.

### AS3_LLM_NETWORKED — OPTIONAL

Only after AS2 passes historical sanity gates.

Agents receive bounded numeric peer-intention summaries over a frozen synthetic network. No free-form persuasion messages and no arbitrary network coefficients presented as empirical truth.

## Data-power gate — MUST PASS BEFORE ANY REAL LLM VOTE

Stop with `ASV2_DATA_POWER_INSUFFICIENT` unless all of the following hold:

- 92/92 constituency-to-demographic crosswalk;
- 92/92 previous-election political anchors;
- age + sex + urban/rural coverage for 92/92;
- education + activity with explicit fallback provenance;
- <=20% of any constituency weight from national fallback;
- <=0.5 pp maximum marginal reconstruction error;
- >=64 effective weighted archetypes per constituency;
- no archetype >10% of constituency weight;
- >=70 districts with >=3 verified candidate identities;
- >=50 districts with >=2 non-trivial context feature families beyond list presence / evidence count.

This gate exists specifically to prevent a repeat of E_reason: a sophisticated experiment around an information set with almost no discriminating power.

## LLM execution

Primary model: `OPUS-5`.

- one model version for the entire arm;
- one frozen prompt;
- deterministic primary pass;
- batches of 32 archetypes;
- every archetype judged independently;
- retry only schema-invalid output with the identical prompt;
- no semantic feedback between attempts;
- all attempts logged and hashed.

Before touching real historical packets, test only the output contract on artificial dummy packets. Once real benchmark output is seen, prompt repair is forbidden.

## Aggregation

The point forecast is the weighted sum of:

`weight × P(turnout) × P(party | turnout)`.

Then generate 20,000 whole-election ballot realizations from the frozen probabilities using common random numbers across comparable arms.

Seats are derived only with the existing frozen legal allocator.

No post-output reweighting to make the result look plausible.

## Scoring

Primary:

`macro_party_share_RMSE` over the frozen 92 x 9 panel.

Secondary:

- mean constituency L1;
- top-party accuracy;
- turnout MAE;
- national local-seat MAE;
- mean energy score.

Uncertainty comparison:

- paired constituency bootstrap;
- 10,000 replicates;
- seed `260818`.

## Historical GO / KILL

2016 and 2021 are diagnostics, not pristine proof.

AS2 may proceed to a frozen 2026 forecast only if:

- output-contract validity >=99%;
- AS2 is not >1% worse than C0 on primary RMSE in either historical year;
- AS2 improves primary RMSE by >=1% in at least one historical year;
- true-context AS2 beats its shuffled-context negative control.

Otherwise: `ASV2_KILL_BEFORE_2026`.

## Prospective 2026 verdict

After the real 2026 election, Agent Society demonstrates agentic alpha only if AS2 vs F0 simultaneously achieves:

- >=2% relative primary-RMSE improvement;
- >=95% paired-bootstrap probability of improvement;
- <=1% relative degradation in mean energy score;
- <=2-seat degradation in national local-seat MAE;
- <=1 pp absolute degradation in top-party accuracy.

If AS3 was run, it additionally requires:

- >=0.5% relative RMSE improvement vs AS2;
- >=90% bootstrap probability of improvement.

Terminal states:

- `ASV2_AGENTIC_ALPHA_DEMONSTRATED`
- `ASV2_NO_AGENTIC_ALPHA`
- `ASV2_DATA_POWER_INSUFFICIENT`
- `ASV2_LEAKAGE_INVALIDATED`
- `ASV2_EXECUTION_BLOCKED`

## Hard boundary

F0 remains the official Atlas forecast throughout the experiment.

Before election day, ASV2 may be shown only as an experimental parallel forecast. It never silently modifies F0.
