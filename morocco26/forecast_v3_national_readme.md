# National forecast V1 execution boundary

The numeric 2026 national point is selected only by the frozen rolling-origin tournament in `scripts/select_national_model_2026_v1.py` under the pre-scoring contracts in `data/goal100/forecast_lab/`.

Current-only polls, political reporting, candidate movement, partial elections, turnout intentions and prediction markets cannot alter the point unless their information class is separately reconstructed historically and wins an out-of-sample admission test.

A selected national point is not a promoted probabilistic forecast. National uncertainty, conditional territorial uncertainty, the legal allocator and end-to-end interval coverage remain separate gates.
