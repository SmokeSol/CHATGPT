Read `AGENT_SOCIETY_FORECAST_V4_PROTOCOL.json` and the V4 goal/state before changing this layer.

## Core invariant

Agent Society is a **behavioural-delta engine**, not a synthetic polling institute. The forecast remains Atlas structural baseline plus a historically calibrated agentic delta.

## Autonomous workflow

1. Pin an exact `main` commit; never read floating `main` during a vintage.
2. Run `inventory-main`, then the candidate/program/territory/registered-electorate adapters.
3. Normalize source-supported facts into a dated vintage spec.
4. Preserve missing candidacies as `UNKNOWN`; never invent a candidate. `NO_LIST` requires evidence and is not a selectable ballot option.
5. Require provenance for substantive program axes.
6. Build registered-electorate cells and deterministic information diets.
7. Generate separate LOCAL and REGIONAL judgments; split-ticket voting is allowed.
8. Social rounds change awareness/credibility/salience/viability/perceptions, never vote probabilities directly.
9. Aggregate the society as a log-ratio/turnout delta against Atlas.
10. Keep lambda at zero until the registered historical sequence: 2016 development → freeze → 2021 holdout.
11. Do not allocate/publish seats without a certified Morocco-2026 rule configuration.
12. Do not scale beyond the named pilot automatically.

## Historical boundary

2016 and 2021 outcomes remain sealed while generating model judgments. The rich historical packets reuse the existing Main Bridge evidence without duplicating it. `TOTAL_BLIND_CONTROL` remains a distinct ablation control.
